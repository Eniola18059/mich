About Myself

My name is Daniel Adeyemi, and I am thirteen years old. I was born on the fifth of March, and I am in Grade 8 at Creative Minds. I live in Lagos, Nigeria, with my parents and my two younger sisters. I am writing this essay to tell you more about who I am. I hope you will enjoy reading it.

My family is very important to me. My father is a civil engineer, and my mother is a nurse. They work hard every day to give us a good life. My sisters are called Tola and Ife, and we enjoy playing board games together on weekends. At home, I help by washing the plates and keeping my room neat.

In my free time, I love playing football with my friends. My favourite team is Arsenal, and my favourite player is Bukayo Saka. I also enjoy drawing and reading adventure stories. At school, my best subject is Mathematics because I like solving problems. I am not very good at Spelling, but I practise a little every night to improve. My favourite teacher is Miss Grace, who always encourages me.

I have many good friends at school, and we help each other with our homework. My best friend is Chidi, and we have known each other since we were seven. A normal school day for me begins at six o'clock in the morning. I wake up, pray, take my bath, and eat breakfast before my father drives me to school. After school, I do my homework, watch a little television, and go to bed early. I believe that honesty and respect are the most important values a person can have, and I try to practise them every day of my life.

I have many dreams for my future. When I grow up, I want to become a doctor so that I can help sick people and make my community healthier. To reach this goal, I must study hard, read many books, and never give up when things become difficult. I also want to learn how to write computer programs because technology is changing the world. In the next five years, I hope to finish secondary school with excellent grades and win a place at a good university. I am a kind, curious, and hardworking boy, and I believe I can achieve anything if I work for it. Thank you for reading about me.
