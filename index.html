Fill in the blanks to write an equivalent expression: 5x + (9 + 24) = ( __ + __ ) + 24
24914x9x5x14
5x + (9 + 24) = (5x + 9) + 24
  1. The 24 is already outside the brackets on the right, so the terms 5x and 9 must go inside.
  2. The numbers stay in the same order (5x, then 9, then 24). Only the grouping moves.
  3. Unused boxes: 24, 14x, 9x, 14.

Your sheet says "commutative", but moving the brackets while keeping the order is technically the associative property. The answer is the same either way.

9t + 33
  1. Distribute the 10: 10(t + 3) = 10t + 30.
  2. The expression becomes 10t + 30 − t + 3.
  3. t terms: 10t − t = 9t. Constants: 30 + 3 = 33.

A package with 12 cans of soda costs $5.88. What is the price per can?
$0.49 per can (49 cents)
  1. Price per can = total price ÷ number of cans = 5.88 ÷ 12.
  2. 12 does not go into 5, so write 0 in the ones place and add the decimal point: 0.
  3. 12 into 58 goes 4 times (12 × 4 = 48). 58 − 48 = 10. Write 4.
  4. Bring down the 8 to make 108. 12 into 108 goes 9 times (12 × 9 = 108). Remainder 0. Write 9.
  5. Answer: 0.49.
