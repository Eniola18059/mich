Quick reminders
  • Coefficient: the number multiplied by a variable (in 7x, it is 7).
  • Term: a part of an expression separated by + or − (3x + 5y − 7 has 3 terms).
  • Constant: a number with no variable. Variable: a letter standing for a number.
  • Exponent: how many times the base is multiplied by itself (2³ = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8).
  • PEMDAS: Parentheses, Exponents, then Multiply/Divide (left to right), then Add/Subtract (left to right).
